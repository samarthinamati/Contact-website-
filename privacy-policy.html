Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

PujariGo respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and shared when you use the PujariGo mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use PujariGo, we may collect:

  • Name and contact information, such as phone number and email address.
  • Account and profile information.
  • Booking details, puja selections and service-related information.
  • Delivery information, such as address, city and PIN code.
  • Store order details and product selections.
  • Seller application information, such as shop name and business contact details.
  • Messages exchanged through the app's chat features.
  • Payment transaction details and payment status.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Process puja and pujari bookings.
  • Process store orders and arrange product delivery.
  • Coordinate customers, sellers and service providers.
  • Process and verify payments.
  • Provide order updates, booking updates and customer support.
  • Improve the app, prevent misuse and maintain security.

3. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers, including Razorpay. Payment information is handled according to the payment provider's privacy and security practices. PujariGo does not store users' UPI PINs or banking passwords.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share relevant information when necessary with:

  • Pujaris or service providers to fulfil bookings.
  • Approved sellers to process and fulfil store orders.
  • Courier and delivery partners to deliver products.
  • Payment providers to process transactions.
  • Technical service providers that help operate the app.
  • Authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

We do not sell users' personal information.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

6. Location and Delivery Information

Delivery addresses and related contact details may be used to fulfil store orders. If the app requests device location access in a feature, the purpose of that access will be explained when permission is requested.

7. Your Choices and Data Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

8. Children's Privacy

PujariGo is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

9. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for payments, hosting, communication and delivery. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
