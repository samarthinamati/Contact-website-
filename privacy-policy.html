Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PujariGo (“we”, “our” or “us”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the PujariGo mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Name, phone number, email address and account/profile details.
  • Booking information, selected puja services and related communications.
  • Delivery details such as recipient name, phone number, address, city and PIN code.
  • Store order details, selected products and order status.
  • Seller application details, such as shop name, owner/contact details and business or pickup address.
  • Messages sent through in-app chat features.
  • Payment transaction references, method and payment status. Payment credentials such as UPI PINs are handled by the payment provider, not requested by PujariGo.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts; arrange pujari bookings; process store orders; coordinate sellers, pujaris and customers; facilitate payments; arrange delivery; provide customer support and updates; maintain app security; and improve our services.

3. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers, including Razorpay. Payment processing is subject to the provider’s terms and privacy policy. PujariGo may receive transaction references and payment status needed to confirm orders or bookings. Do not share your UPI PIN, banking password or OTP with anyone.

4. When We Share Information

We share only information relevant to the service being provided, where necessary, with:

  • Pujaris or service providers to fulfil bookings.
  • Relevant sellers to prepare and fulfil store orders.
  • Courier and delivery partners to deliver orders.
  • Payment providers to process and verify transactions.
  • Hosting, technology and support providers that help operate PujariGo.
  • Government authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by law.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Data Storage, Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, handle disputes, meet legal/accounting requirements and enforce our agreements.

6. Location and Delivery

Address and contact details supplied during checkout may be used to fulfil deliveries. If a feature requests device-location permission, the purpose will be presented as part of the permission request. You can manage device permissions through your device settings.

7. Your Choices and Data Requests

You may contact us to request access to or correction or deletion of personal information associated with your account. We may ask for information to verify your identity. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate transaction, safety or dispute-resolution purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

PujariGo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the request.

9. Third-Party Services

PujariGo may rely on third-party services for payment processing, hosting, communication and delivery. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Please review the relevant provider policies where applicable.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or practices change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
